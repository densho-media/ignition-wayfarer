## Concepts that failed or were set aside

| ID | Concept | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X-ACC | Accelerator-driven selective fusion | Coulomb scattering outnumbers fusion about 60 to 1; recovery would need 99.997 %. |
| X-AMPN | Broad plasma dust shield (AMPN) | Its power and mass exceed what it protects; passive screens are lighter. |
| X-REMOTE | Kilometre-scale remote engines (MPN F, RON B-E, Halo) | They fit the heat but not the compact silhouette or the mass. |
| X-FULLC | Full coil blanket (2,147 t per engine) | Moves heat into lithium but does not remove it; far too heavy. |
| X-A | Reflective coatings on the drawn faces (A) | The faces meet the burn at about 60°; a coating returns 1e-5 of the X-rays. |
| X-D | Deflector liners inside the engine (D) | The turn needed is far beyond any critical angle. |
| X-EXTR | Early extraction as a thrust lever | Escaped protons carry energy but almost no momentum; it lowers X-rays and heat, not thrust. |
| X-MULT | Multiplying the topology and extraction gains | Computed together, extraction removes the momentum the topology won: 0.36 MN, not 0.94 MN, at about 230x. |
| X-FIELD | More field instead of a better topology | Hit shares do not move with field strength; only the capture energy and the coil problems grow. |
| X-SMALL | Smaller, more frequent pulses to cut the waste heat | At fixed power the shares do not move with target mass: heat and thrust stay; only per-pulse loads fall. |
| X-H2UP | Outside hydrogen to take the product energy | The fuel already stops the products; added mass lowers an exhaust speed already below the need. |
