## ENG-001: Whole-ship mass does not reach the 4,426 t benchmark

- Variant: AMPN B today
- Mechanism: Magnet, protection and radiator mass raise dry mass; eta 0.86 and canon delta-v set the mass ratio
- Evidence: `lab/studies/vertex-compact-engine/vertex-ampn/results/trade.json; tables.md`
- Assumptions and uncertainty: Canon jet power and four 4,797 km/s burns; REBCO 1.53e8 A/m2
- Possible solutions: Higher Je tape (9,820 t at 6e8), lighter supports, eta toward 0.95, mission-level trade (owner)
- Required advances: Conductor Je >= 6e8 A/m2 at 20 K with 3e22 n/m2 tolerance
- Next experiment or simulation: Mission-level trade study; tape sensitivity
- Model components: ENGINE, SHIP

## ENG-002: Field-ripple heating of the winding and eddy shield

- Variant: AMPN B today
- Mechanism: 20 kHz pulses couple a field ripple dB/B ~ eps_c E/(2W) into the winding; Bean AC loss and eddy heating
- Evidence: `lab/studies/vertex-compact-engine/vertex-ampn/results/audit.json (ripple rows), coilpack.json`
- Assumptions and uncertainty: Coupling eps_c 0.3 (range 0.1-1): not measured
- Possible solutions: Pulse shaping, flux-conserving inner shell, larger W/E ratio, segmented eddy shield
- Required advances: Measured coupling
- Next experiment or simulation: Plasma-coupled pulse simulation (resistive MHD) for eps_c
- Model components: RING, EDDY_SHIELD
