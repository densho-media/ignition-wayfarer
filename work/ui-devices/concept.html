Crew Interfaces

The public site should look and behave like the hardware the crew actually uses. On a phone you are holding a personal device: the crew cuff on your forearm, or a service slate. On a desktop you are standing or sitting at a ship station: an OPS console or an engineering workstation. What the screen offers follows the activity, the way it would aboard: you check yourself and your route on your wrist, and you run the ship from a station.

Crew cuff
CU · wrist unit, left forearm		PERSONAL Every crew member. The instructor already wears one (docs/CHARACTERS.md); the newcomer is issued one at boarding. Who and where am I, my next task, local air and alerts, intercom, route to a compartment. One hand, often gloved: targets ≥ 11 mm, one task per screen, three hard keys (BACK · HOME · ALERT). Low power, dims for the night cycle. Every page at phone portrait; HUD of the interior walk.

Glanceable, consumables first, fixed rows, no scrolling. Chest labels are printed mirror-reversed to read in the wrist mirror, as on real EVA suits. HUD of Explore Exterior (every screen size).
OPS console

Run the ship: buses and breakers, load shedding, air, crew orders. Deadship scenarios already send the player here. Keyboard and pointer, multi-pane, dense text, F-key soft-key row under the screen, a ship status strip that never leaves. Start page, Technical status and Deadship panels on a desktop.
Engineering workstation
records and design station		STATIONAL PROTOTYPE placement: an OPS station in engineering mode (no separate room is sourced). Read design records, compare variants, inspect the model with its source status. Wide screen, three panes (index · document · context), keyboard navigation. Engineering pages and Explore Ship on a desktop.
Burn-go console
armrest of every couch		STATIONAL Burn station couches (docs/MODEL.md: a burn-go console on each armrest, 0.72–0.86 m). Under thrust, strapped in: acknowledge, hold, go. Few large keys reachable without lifting the arm against the load. Later: the First Ignition / burn scenes only.

Class follows the activity, not only the screen. Walking the ship is personal even on a desktop (your cuff), and sitting down at an OPS chair in the walk is stational even on a phone. Screen size only picks the default frame for the site pages.

The start page on each device

Design rules for the hardware look

Function first (HSFP)

  • A casing, a nameplate and hard keys appear because the device has them. No glow, glass, scan lines or decorative circuitry (docs/WEB_IDENTITY.md).
  • Every on-screen value is real site data or labelled. MET, ring g and bus state come from the model or say SIMULATION ONLY.

Same identity

  • WEB-005 colours stay: tokens from tools/web_identity.py, the four colour layers, the lunar scene behind the casing.
  • One new token pair, casing and nameplate, added in the generator, never in CSS.

Usable and light

  • 7:1 text contrast, focus rings, keyboard paths and axe-core clean, as now.
  • Pure CSS and HTML: the start page still loads only index.html, site.css and release.json. No change to the Godot or three.js frame budgets.
