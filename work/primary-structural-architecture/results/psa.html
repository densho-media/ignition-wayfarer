G.U. WAYFARER 001 · PSA · Phase I for owner review, 2026-11-28 · not canon

Primary Structural Architecture

Phase I asks what structure can carry Wayfarer's thrust to every mass ahead of it, be built at Luna Yard from mostly lunar material, and be repaired in flight. The drive is power-limited, so the section loads stay near 21 MN whether the ship weighs 4.5 kt or 12.9 kt. Against those loads, all-aluminium structure is about 240 t. Only multi-material zoning or moving the tanks aft gets close to the 150 t line. The study changes no canon, Drive data, production geometry or budget. It is built on main 11f1e94 and ISSA 5eae701.

OWNER REVIEW GATE

What holds, and what to reconsider

The existing design gets right

  • The topology: thrust enters through 4 nozzles, a 12-node ring and an 8 m transition into 3 sectors plus the spine.
  • The 4 m frames. At this pitch, bay buckling governs titanium and composite rails, not strength.
  • Kinematic mounting of the blades and tanks (one datum plus slides). It must extend to every long system.
  • The Q45 bypass collar around the rotor bay.
  • Locking the rings before thrust, so their spin torque never enters the frame.

Needs reconsidering

  • The design load. The canon rule of 1.5 g on the full ship asks for 3× what the drive can push.
  • Nozzle pickups at 78.7°. They turn each 7.35 MN share into 37.7 MN per strut. Re-angled, each strut carries ~1.9 MN.
  • 3 single rails. One cut removes a third of the area, so PSA proposes 6 paired keels.
  • Tank shells booked inside the 150 t line while 458 t of tankage is modelled outside it.
  • The radiator blade backbone. It runs at 1650-1800 K, so it belongs to thermal and must be made of refractory material.
1 · LOADS

Compression along the ship

The load at each station is N(x) = T · mahead(x) / M, the inertial reaction of everything forward of x. Masses are placed by mass_model for P and by ISSA district for F1 and R. Configurations are never mixed.

Restricted

2xxx, 7xxx and Al-Li alloys need Cu, Zn or Li imports, and Al-Li is kept for tank shells. Steels are heavy and ferromagnetic, so none goes where |∇B| exceeds 10⁻³ T/m. Mg has flammability and creep problems. Additive parts need ISM-33 qualification before they enter a primary path.

Excluded

Fiji is not a structural material. Canon says it cannot be synthesised, and it is reserved for the drive.
