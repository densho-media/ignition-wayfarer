Integrated Ship Systems Architecture

Phase VI makes the two development tracks permanent and keeps them apart. Track R (research) holds today's engineering; Track F (fictional) is the idealized G.U. WAYFARER 001 and may go beyond today's engineering but never beyond physics, with every step named in a technology register. Phase VI reconciles the Phase V figures, writes one machine-readable configuration per baseline, adds the 1500 K turbine (FT-03) as the Track F reference, accounts for direct conversion as a conditional option, settles life support on seven units and runs the next OpenMC research steps. It changes no canon, Drive data or production geometry. Phases V to I follow below, kept for the record.

Recommended direction: D, a three-level hybrid (not a final selection)

L1 · Burn power

Generated from the drive's hot loop while it runs: burn heat pumps (210 MW at burnout), hot-loop pumps, Burn Watch, recharge. It needs its own budget line in the drive package.

L2 · Ship service

Two independent fission-Brayton plants as the working baseline, each able to carry essential loads alone. Auxiliary fusion (C) stays a speculative alternative. In coast they reject to the dark blades at 550–650 K.

L3 · Survival

An isolated kWe source (RPS or small fission-Stirling) plus batteries, near the refuge, sharing no bus or loop with L2. It keeps the crew and, if possible, the cryocoolers alive.

Main-drive hotel mode (A) is not credible as the continuous source: one machine would have to hold a burning plasma at 10⁻⁴ to 10⁻⁵ of its rating for six years, and every drive fault would become a ship-power fault. Cryocooling (about 0.4 MW) is mission-critical: uncooled He-3 boils off at about {{HE3}} per day.

G · PARAMETRIC INTEGRATION

A systems registry that extends what exists

The repository already has the pieces of a registry: data/modules.toml owners, physics.mass_budget and mass_regions, exterior_hardware.inertia_items, the SYSTEM_GROUPS 11–16 with their colours, object custom properties (canon_status, source_ref, engineering_status), the ring-services equipment and LRU tables, the drone fleet and the representation zones. The proposal joins them rather than adding a framework.

  1. data/systems.toml (DR-13): one row per assembly, as in section C, with budget naming a mass_budget key and district naming a placement envelope.
  2. [power.loads]: PROTOTYPE load bands per item and mode, read by a pure power_loads.py (first task, K).
  3. Envelopes before parts: each placed system gets a SYS_<id> envelope object in its system group, carrying sys_id, maturity and status as custom properties, so inspect can check clearances and the website can colour by maturity.
  4. Checks: budget sums against mass_budget, every envelope inside a district, every powered row on a bus. Tests stay limited to owner numbers and physical invariants.

Website (WOS) ideas

A Systems section in the Engineering module: the gap matrix with maturity chips, a mode selector that shows the load table and which buses carry it, and the district view coloured by maturity. Values come from wos.json; OPEN values show as OPEN.

H · BUDGETS

Is 50 t enough for power, computers, steering and comms?

Only for ship service with no defense duty, and barely. The item already holds this hardware:

{{PC_ROWS}}

Already used{{PC_USED}}

Two ship-service fission-Brayton plants need about 24 t nominal before shadow shields. With the proactive defense band they need about 107 t, and with the normal active band 400–1,500 t. The proposal is an explicit new line in the budget once DR-01 sets the defense duty, not a silent increase of the 50 t item (DR-04).

Other lines to reconcile: food for 12 (about 48 t against 10 t, DR-07); burn generation (no line, proposed under the drive package); shadow shields (no line); dry-store contents (volume is plentiful, mass is not).

J · OWNER DECISIONS

Decision register

  1. DR-01Coast defense duty. Is the normal active band (15–30 MW) continuous for the whole cruise, or triggered? Recommend: proactive continuous, normal active as a triggered mode. This decides whether the coast plant is tens or hundreds of tonnes.
  2. DR-02Power architecture. Adopt three levels (D) with fission-Brayton as the working baseline for L2? Recommend: yes, with auxiliary fusion kept as a speculative alternative.
  3. DR-03Blade reuse. May coast plants reject heat to the dark hot blades at 550–650 K? Recommend: yes; no new radiator and no silhouette change.
  4. DR-04Power budget. Add an explicit drive-off generation line instead of growing the 50 t item? Recommend: yes, sized after DR-01.
  5. DR-05Survival endurance. How long must L3 keep the crew alive, and with or without cryocoolers? Recommend: 30 days crew-only as a working target.
  6. DR-06Cryo priority. Treat cryocoolers as mission-critical with n+1 units on two buses? Recommend: yes.
  7. DR-07Food. Raise the food line to about 48 t for 12, or credit the garden? Recommend: stored food at full rate as the baseline, garden as margin.
  8. DR-08Shield water as makeup. May the forward shield water feed ECLSS losses? Recommend: only with a minimum-shielding floor.
  9. DR-09RCS propellant. Owner and budget line for RCS propellant over six years.
  10. DR-10Machinery access. Unpressurized ORUs serviced by drones and EVA, or pressurized engine rooms? Recommend: unpressurized ORUs, as on ISS.
  11. DR-11Braking-direction dust. Which system protects the ship when the drive points forward?
  12. DR-12Dry store. Repurpose modules M1–M4 for power machinery and M8–M9 for food and spares? Recommend: yes.
  13. DR-13Registry. Adopt data/systems.toml as the systems registry? Recommend: yes, starting with EPS and TMS.
