ENTER WAYFARER

Integrated Demonstrator 01: one continuous run from the docked exterior through the airlock, the microgravity service spine and the transfer drum, down the ladder into ring A's spin gravity. The camera guides each chapter; you can take over the crew body in the physics chapters.

The engine and the ship package download when you press Start.

Controls

  • Keyboard: Enter next chapter · P pause the guided camera · F2 autoplay the body actions · Esc back. The chapter text shows the keys that work now; F10 opens the controls.
  • Touch: left thumb moves where the body can walk or climb, right thumb drags to look, buttons appear when their action can be used (Grab, Push off, Next …); ? explains them. Landscape works best.
  • ← Exit leaves and unloads the engine.

About this build

  • Browser build of the native Godot project: same scenes, scripts, physics (Jolt) and DOCKED package; single-threaded WebGL 2.
  • The induction pair appears as proxy figures; the native development runtime carries the full characters.
  • SIMULATION_ONLY: chapters, camera paths and the ring-ramp time-lapse are display choices; no transfer timing, procedure or interlock is released.
  • Tested in Chromium-based desktop browsers and in Chrome's phone and tablet emulation with touch; real phones, Firefox and Safari are not yet verified. On phones the 3-D view renders at a reduced, frame-rate-adaptive resolution.
  • Built with the Godot Engine, MIT licence: licence, third-party notices.
