ENTER WAYFARER

The walk: start in the development station, board Wayfarer as the trainee, cross into a habitat ring and walk its whole circumference through every sector and corridor, then return to the station. One continuous run under your control: you work the airlock, ask the ship to undock and spin its rings up, ride the transfer drum, climb down into spin gravity, open the doors and come back the same way.

The engine and the ship package download when you press Start.

Desktop: click to look (Esc frees the cursor) · W/S move, A/D step · E use (hatch, door, drum, ladder, the ring B branch) · O ship operations at a trunk panel · V view · M ring map. Touch: left thumb moves, right thumb drags to look, the Use, Ops, View and Map buttons appear when they apply.

Controls

  • The walk: the goal at the top left says what to do next and which key or button does it; the ring map shows the stretch walked. A shut hatch or door stops you; open it first. F10 opens the controls.
  • Touch: left thumb moves where the body can walk or climb, right thumb drags to look, buttons appear when their action can be used (Use, Ops, View, Map); ? explains them. Landscape works best.
  • ← Exit leaves and unloads the engine.

About this build

  • Browser build of the native Godot project: same scenes, scripts, physics (Jolt) and DOCKED package; single-threaded WebGL 2.
  • The trainee appears as a proxy body; the native development runtime carries the full characters.
  • SIMULATION_ONLY: the ring-ramp time-lapse and the closure timings are display choices; no transfer timing, procedure or interlock is released.
  • Tested in Chromium-based desktop browsers and in Chrome's phone and tablet emulation with touch; real phones, Firefox and Safari are not yet verified. On phones the 3-D view renders at a reduced, frame-rate-adaptive resolution.
