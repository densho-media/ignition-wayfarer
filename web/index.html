About this project

Wayfarer is a fictional spacecraft from the IGNITION world, worked out with real physics and engineering methods. It is an evolving design, not a real or operating ship: some problems are solved, others are still open.

What the labels mean

The 3D ship and the experiences are simulation-only representations of a proxy model (REP_PROXY). Every value carries a status: CANON and DERIVED come from the design workbooks, WORKING and PROTOTYPE are choices still under review, DIAGNOSTIC values come from simulations and HOLD marks what is not cleared. Nothing here is flight data or a certified procedure.
