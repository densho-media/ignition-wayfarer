EXPLORE EXTERIOR

Step outside Wayfarer. You start standing on a magnetic training walkway on the forward hull collar, beside the EVA rail, in a Y155 suit with the companion drone docked on your back and your tether clipped to a rail anchor. The ship is drifting, so nothing pulls you down: only your boot magnets hold you to the plate. Walk on them, then switch them off or push off and fly with the suit's cold-gas thrusters along the hull and the Brow. The hatch start and the guided training flight are one click away.

The engine and the ship package download when you press Start.

Controls

  • Magnetic boots: W/S walk forward or back (one boot always stays on the plate) · A/D side step · J/L turn · V boot magnets on or off (off releases both boots at once) · Space release and push off the plate with your legs. The walkway's yellow edge is its end: the boots will not step off it.
  • Flying: W/S A/D R/F thrust forward/back, left/right, up/down · I/K J/L U/O pitch, yaw, roll · Shift fine thrust · B brake (assisted) · M manual or assisted · H attitude hold · P position hold · right mouse drag looks, the wheel zooms · C next camera.
  • On the hull: G grab or release the hold in reach (rail anchors, Brow fixtures, the station rack) · Space push off along your view · T clip or unclip the tether · E use or inspect what is in reach.
  • The drone: 1 control the astronaut, 2 the drone (the other keeps moving under physics) · X release the drone or call it back to dock · Y its mode (manual, hold, follow, return to astronaut, return to ship) · Z arms · N gripper · Q ask it for nitrogen.
  • Training: F4 starts or leaves the guided "EVA — First Flight" · F5 training reset (back to your start and every vessel refilled, labelled; R is a thruster key here) · F6 last checkpoint · F2 drift or burn presets · F3 operational rules · F7 a valve fault.
  • Back and exit: fly back to the hatch and grip its hold to end where you began. ← Exit (bottom left) leaves and unloads the engine; your browser's Back button does the same. F8 goes inside, to Explore Interior, in the same session.

About this experience

  • EVA Operations (EVA-OPS-001): the suited astronaut and the companion drone, both live in one fixed-step simulation. Cold-gas thrusters with their real nozzle layout and allocation, finite nitrogen in four inventories, momentum that only gas can take away, contact with the hull, holds and tether. The ship is the current generated model.
  • Where you can go: the forward collar, the EVA rail, the Brow's faces, rim and truss, the airlock fitting and the station rack have holds and colliders. The drive, radiator and other engineering areas are not EVA-cleared: the keep-outs and the ram face are enforced and warned on the HUD, and the training reset is the only way back from a mistake.
  • TRAINING SIMULATION: flight EVA stays HOLD in the canon (S&E 45 O-07). Every hold is SIMULATION_ONLY and nothing here is a flight procedure. The suit is the Y155 model on the project's MPFB instructor; its clips follow the physics, never the reverse.
  • Magnetic boots are a PROTOTYPE (docs/LOCOMOTION.md): each boot's pad pulls with a bounded 200 N on the steel walkway only (not on the hull skin), its grip is that pull times a sole friction of 0.5, and a stronger shear or pull makes it slip or break away. The walkway is drawn by the simulation and is not part of the engineering model.
  • Keyboard and mouse only, desktop browser; key labels show US key positions. Tested in Chromium-based browsers.
